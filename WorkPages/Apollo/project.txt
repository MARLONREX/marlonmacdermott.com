Title: Apollo
Year: 2024
Category: Photography
Cover: 01-covered-passage.jpg

Designed by Victor Pasmore and completed in 1969, the Apollo Pavilion in Peterlee is a reinforced concrete sculpture and bridge. Its interlocking blocks connect the geometry of the estate’s curving roads and box-shaped houses. As you move around it, the concrete forms seem to rotate and shift, changing the shape you see from each new angle.
